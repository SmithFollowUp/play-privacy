BatchWorks — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Local storage

BatchWorks stores records, associated photos, status information and application history on your device. We do not collect or receive this information.

Collection and sharing

The application has no cloud accounts, advertisements, third-party analytics, data transmission or background tracking. We do not share your stored data with third parties.

Exports and backups

Exporting a report, CSV or backup saves a copy to a location that you select. You control how and with whom you share the file.

Deleting data

Records can be deleted within the app. Uninstalling the app deletes its application storage. Previously exported files remain wherever you saved them.

Permissions

The app does not request Android runtime permissions. File selection and exports are controlled through Android's system file picker.

Contact

For support, contact the developer through the support contact on the Google Play listing.
